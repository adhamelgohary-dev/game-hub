### Team routing

- **Orchestrator** sequences milestones, integrates results, keeps `PROGRESS.md` and `HANDOFF.md` current, and convenes the Review Council.
- **Architect**, **Software Designer**, and **Data & Extensibility Designer** update `docs/design/` and `DECISIONS.md` before code changes.
- **Library**, **Sessions & Capture**, **Audio/Notes/Hotkeys**, **Protection**, and **UI** implementers build against agreed contracts.
- **Security & Privacy**, **UX & Accessibility**, **QA**, **Tutor**, and **Integration & Packaging** audit, test, document (`LEARNING.md`), and package every milestone.
- **Review Council** (Advocate, Critic, Neutral Analyst, Judge) gates `D-v1.1` and every milestone (`M9`–`M12`) in `docs/reviews/`.
